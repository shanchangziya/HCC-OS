# English figure legends

## Figure 1. Definition and sample distribution of oxidative-stress strata in the discovery epithelial-cell collection

**(A)** Data provenance and the composite oxidative-stress (OS) score. The saved GSE202642 discovery object contains 10,413 epithelial cells from seven tumor and four adjacent tissue samples; tissue samples are not assumed to represent 11 distinct patients. The composite is the arithmetic mean of cohort-standardized AddModuleScore, UCell and AUCell ROS scores. The historical saved high group contains 2,437 cells, whereas a revised cohort-wide 80th-percentile definition contains 2,083 cells. **(B)** Saved discovery UMAP colored by the composite score. **(C)** The same embedding colored by the historical saved groups, retained to document original program/model provenance (OS-low, 7,976; OS-high, 2,437). **(D)** Composite-score histogram colored by the historical groups. The dashed line marks the revised 80th percentile (1.0173), not a reconstructed historical density minimum. **(E)** Fraction of high cells per tissue sample under each definition; bar colors encode definitions in this panel. **(F)** Cell-level agreement between historical and revised classifications. Of the historical high cells, 354 are reassigned to low under the revised definition. All plots are descriptive and show the saved discovery collection without excluding adjacent-tissue cells. The collection includes mixed CNV classifications and must not be equated with a uniformly confirmed malignant-cell population. Sample p5 contributes 2,079/2,437 historical high cells (85.31%). The revised labels were not retrospectively substituted into the original OSARS model derivation.

## Figure 2. Independent assessment of the frozen discovery program in GSE149614

**(A)** UMAP of 71,915 cells from 21 tissue samples and 10 patients, colored by author-provided broad cell types. **(B,C)** UMAP of 13,691 primary-tumor cells belonging to the author's malignant hepatocyte clusters, colored by the composite OS score (B) and frozen discovery MP4 UCell score after removing the ROS-overlap genes ATOX1 and TXN (C). The same cells and coordinates are used in B and C. Embeddings were generated using sample-level Harmony correction; gene-set scores were computed from uncorrected normalized RNA. Cohort-level standardization and relative percentile assignment were reapplied within the validation cohort, rather than transferring the numerical discovery cutoff. **(D)** Mean within-patient MP4 score differences between high and low cells under the prespecified cohort-wide composite top-20% definition and two sensitivity definitions: within-patient composite top-20% and within-patient UCell top-20%. Orange denotes frozen MP4 and blue denotes MP4 excluding ROS-overlap genes. Points are equal-weight means across eligible patients; horizontal intervals are 95% percentile bootstrap intervals from 5,000 patient resamples. At least five cells in both strata were required: eight patients contributed to the primary comparison, and ten to each sensitivity analysis. The primary MP4 difference was 0.01465 (95% mean bootstrap interval, −0.01028 to 0.04081; two-sided signed-rank P=0.46094; BH q=0.76823). Within-patient composite and UCell sensitivities had q=0.08138 and q=0.00488, respectively. MP4−ROS primary and sensitivity q values were 0.76823, 0.08138 and 0.00488. BH adjustment was applied to five programs within each definition. Bootstrap intervals summarize means, whereas signed-rank P values test a location null; they are not interchangeable estimates of the same quantity. **(E)** Patient-specific Spearman correlations between composite OS and MP4−ROS scores, before and after partial rank adjustment for library size and mitochondrial percentage. Each row represents one patient. The mean adjusted coefficient was 0.14859; the two-sided signed-rank test across ten patients gave P=0.08398. **(F)** Descriptive gene-set Jaccard indices between frozen discovery and independently derived validation NMF programs. Program numbers are arbitrary across datasets. Discovery MP4 shares 34 genes with validation MP2 (57 and 104 measured genes, respectively; Jaccard=0.26772). No significance symbols are shown. Conditional enrichment tests using the actual 2,000 validation-NMF candidate genes, with both sets restricted to that universe and BH adjustment across 16 comparisons, are provided separately; these tests do not measure patient-level phenotype replication. The primary cohort retains author-selected cells, including 997 cells failing the additional technical-QC thresholds; a 12,694-cell QC sensitivity preserving original scores and labels is reported in the source tables. The primary global definition did not demonstrate donor-level MP4 enrichment; sensitivity and gene-overlap findings should not be interpreted as complete replication of that phenotype.
