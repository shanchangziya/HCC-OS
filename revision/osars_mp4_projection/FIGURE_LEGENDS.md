# Figure legends

**Primary GSEA summary.** Pre-ranked GSEA comparing the frozen OSARS-high and OSARS-low groups in TCGA-LIHC (n = 343) and ICGC-LIRI (n = 243). Genes were ranked by the Welch t statistic for high minus low expression. The frozen discovery MP4 program and a version excluding Hallmark ROS and OSARS-feature overlap are shown alongside Hallmark ROS as a positive-control program. Filled symbols denote BH q < 0.05; blue and orange indicate enrichment toward OSARS-low and OSARS-high, respectively. Negative MP4 normalized enrichment scores indicate enrichment in OSARS-low tumors.

**Stage-adjusted GSEA summary.** Sensitivity analysis using the same gene sets and risk groups after fitting each gene as expression ~ OSARS group + categorical pathological stage, with the adjusted OSARS-high coefficient t statistic used for ranking. TCGA-LIHC includes 321 stage-complete cases; ICGC-LIRI includes 243 cases. The direction of MP4 enrichment remains toward OSARS-low.

**Running-score panels.** GSEA running enrichment scores for the MP4 program after removing ROS and OSARS-feature-overlap genes in TCGA-LIHC and ICGC-LIRI. Vertical ticks identify program genes in the ranked list. The left and right ends of the rank correspond to relative overexpression in OSARS-high and OSARS-low tumors, respectively.
