# Figure legends

**TCGA-LIHC multi-method state scores by OSARS group.** Per-sample projections of the pre-specified MP4 program after excluding ROS and OSARS-feature overlap. The frozen OSARS cohort-median group contains 172 low- and 171 high-risk tumors. Violin plots, boxes and individual observations show the score distributions. Standardized mean differences are high minus low with 2,000 sample-row bootstrap 95% confidence intervals. P values are two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum tests with BH adjustment across the seven calculated scoring methods.

**TCGA-LIHC continuous OSARS association.** Continuous relationships between the frozen OSARS score and the same pre-specified state scores. Colors retain the frozen risk groups. The black line is a descriptive least-squares fit; labeled inference is two-sided Spearman correlation with a 2,000 sample-row bootstrap 95% confidence interval and BH adjustment across seven methods.

**Multi-method replication effects.** Standardized mean differences of state score between OSARS-high and OSARS-low tumors in TCGA-LIHC and ICGC-LIRI. Error bars are 2,000 sample-row bootstrap 95% confidence intervals. Filled symbols denote BH q < 0.05 for the score ~ OSARS group + categorical pathological stage sensitivity model, adjusted across seven scoring methods within cohort. Negative effects indicate lower state scores in OSARS-high tumors.
