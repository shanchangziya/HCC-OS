# Revised figure legends

**Figure 5. OSARS associations with inferred immune features and TIDE predictions in TCGA-LIHC.** A, Standardized mean differences in 11 prespecified immune and stromal features from the archived analysis. Differences are calculated as the high-group mean minus the low-group mean divided by the standard deviation across all 343 samples. Horizontal intervals are unadjusted 95% percentile bootstrap intervals from 5,000 within-group resamples. CIB, CIBERSORT; EST, ESTIMATE; ssGSEA, single-sample gene-set enrichment analysis. B, Individual values of the six archived TIDE-derived scores. Boxes show the median and interquartile range, whiskers extend to observations within 1.5 times the interquartile range, and points denote individual patients. C, Proportions classified as predicted ICB responders by TIDE, with Wilson 95% confidence intervals; labels give responders/total. The analysis includes 171 OSARS-low and 172 OSARS-high patients. Frozen group assignments use risk score ≥ the TCGA cohort median for OSARS-high. Blue indicates OSARS-low and orange indicates OSARS-high; in A, color indicates the direction of the difference. Two-sided Mann–Whitney tests with continuity and tie corrections were adjusted by the Benjamini–Hochberg method separately across the 11 features in A and the six scores in B. Asterisks denote FDR < 0.05 (*), < 0.01 (**), or < 0.001 (***); ns, FDR ≥ 0.05. The two-sided Fisher exact P value in C is 0.001418. TIDE labels are computational predictions, not observed treatment outcomes. CIBERSORT estimates in A include all archived samples regardless of fit P value; only 36 samples meet P < 0.05, and the corresponding sensitivity analysis is shown separately. These inferred immune associations are exploratory.

**Supplementary Figure [therapy]. OSARS associations with distinct treatment-related endpoints.** A, GEO-deposited TACE response labels among 147 patients with HCC in GSE104580, with 51/74 OSARS-low and 30/73 OSARS-high samples labeled as responders (two-sided Fisher P = 0.0008926). Expression was obtained from pretreatment tumor biopsies. The original deposited response categories are retained; imaging responses were not reassessed. B, The original BIOSTORM recurrence-free-survival-benefit signature class among 67 sorafenib-treated patients in GSE109211: 15/34 OSARS-low and 6/33 OSARS-high cases carry the responder label (P = 0.03444). The cohort concerns adjuvant treatment after resection, and these labels were defined by the original recurrence-benefit gene signature; they do not represent tumor shrinkage. All 67 labels were checked against original GEO annotations. C, RECIST response to adoptive transfer of tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes in 21 patients with melanoma in GSE100797: 6/11 OSARS-low and 2/10 OSARS-high patients had complete or partial response, compared with stable or progressive disease (P = 0.1827). This is exploratory cross-cancer evidence, not HCC CAR-T validation. Points show proportions, intervals indicate Wilson 95% confidence intervals, and fractions report responders/total. Each cohort uses its eligible-sample median, with risk score > median defining OSARS-high. The frozen 128-feature, 1,696-tree GBM was used; absent genes were imputed with TCGA training means. Benjamini–Hochberg FDR values across these three exploratory comparisons are 0.002678, 0.05166, and 0.1827, respectively. Neither OSARS-by-treatment interactions nor causal treatment benefit were evaluated.

**Supplementary Figure [CIBERSORT sensitivity]. Dependence of inferred CIBERSORT associations on fit filtering.** A, Treg fraction differences. B, Macrophage M0 fraction differences. Estimates are the OSARS-high minus OSARS-low mean fraction, expressed in percentage points, with unadjusted 95% percentile bootstrap intervals from 5,000 within-group patient resamples. Filled circles include all 343 archived estimates (171 low, 172 high); open circles retain only the 36 samples with CIBERSORT fit P < 0.05 (9 low, 27 high). The threshold is the CIBERSORT deconvolution fit P value, not the group-comparison P value. Labels report Benjamini–Hochberg FDR from two-sided Mann–Whitney tests across all 22 cell fractions separately within each analysis set. The filtered analyses do not provide statistical confirmation of the all-sample Treg or M0 association and have small, imbalanced groups. Complete results for all fractions are supplied in the source-data table.

**Supplementary Figure [DrugReflector]. Exploratory ranking of compounds from the archived OSARS-high-to-low DrugReflector analysis.** A, The top 20 compounds in the original raw rank order, retaining unnamed compounds by their Broad identifiers. The archived transition signature used all 26,280 available expression features from 343 TCGA-LIHC samples (172 high, 171 low). Values are DrugReflector model output probabilities, not calibrated probabilities of patient response or estimates of drug efficacy. No experimental validation or inferential significance test is represented. The complete top-100 output is supplied as source data. This analysis is hypothesis-generating.

**Supplementary Figure [checkpoints; optional]. Archived immune-checkpoint expression by OSARS group.** A, Eight checkpoint genes in 343 TCGA-LIHC samples, with 171 low and 172 high cases. Individual observations and the archived numeric expression scale are shown. Boxes, whiskers, and group assignments are defined as in Figure 5. Two-sided Mann–Whitney P values were adjusted across all eight genes by the Benjamini–Hochberg method; *, FDR < 0.05; **, FDR < 0.01; ***, FDR < 0.001; ns, FDR ≥ 0.05. The precise expression unit/transformation needs confirmation from the original TCGA preprocessing record before this optional figure is used for submission.

# Primary source verification

- BIOSTORM endpoint definition: [Pinyol et al., Gut 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30108162/), DOI 10.1136/gutjnl-2018-316408; original article XML saved in `data/source/BIOSTORM_original_article.xml`. Its Figure 3 defines the 146-gene recurrence-benefit classification. GEO [GSE109211](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE109211) and all 140 sample annotations were retrieved, including the 67 sorafenib cases used here.
- TIL-ACT indication and response definition: [Lauss et al., Nature Communications 2017](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-017-01460-0), DOI 10.1038/s41467-017-01460-0. The present analysis uses the 21 expression-matched patients and RECIST response, rather than the broader clinical-benefit category used for other analyses in that publication.
- TACE sample origin and deposited response group: [GSE104580 BioProject](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA413131) and [GEO sample GSM2803686](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM2803686). Primary deposited metadata do not specify the imaging adjudication rule, so this legend does not assert an unverified mRECIST definition.
